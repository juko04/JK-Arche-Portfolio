Based in Boulder, Colorado

"Light is both a quantitative architectural material and a biological necessity. Good lighting doesn't just illuminate space—it shapes how we feel, focus, and move through it."

I am an Architectural Engineering graduate student at the University of Colorado Boulder with a passion for architectural lighting design. My work bridges the analytical discipline of building engineering with spatial design and human perception.

Whether studying daylight harvesting and solar trajectories or sculpting nighttime identities through electric luminaire integration, I treat light as an active architectural material. My background in photography further sharpens my eye for contrast, shadow gradients, color temperature, and composition.
