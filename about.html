"Light is not an afterthought added to architecture; it is the medium through which form and space are revealed."

I am an Architectural Engineering student at the University of Colorado Boulder with a specialized passion for architectural lighting design. My work bridges the analytical discipline of building science with the atmospheric sensitivity of spatial design.

Whether studying daylight harvesting and solar trajectories or sculpting nighttime identities through electric luminaire integration, I treat light as an active architectural material. My background in photography further sharpens my eye for contrast, shadow gradients, color temperature, and composition.
