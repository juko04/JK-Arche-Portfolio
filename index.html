ARCHITECTURAL ENGINEERING · FOCUS IN LIGHTING DESIGN CU BOULDER · PORTFOLIO 2026

Julian Kotara

"Light is both a quantitative architectural material and a biological necessity. Good lighting doesn't just illuminate space—it shapes how we feel, focus, and move through it."

I study how daylight and electric light shape human biology, mood, and spatial perception. By balancing computational analysis with an appreciation for shadow and texture, I design environments that feel natural, comfortable, and intentional.
